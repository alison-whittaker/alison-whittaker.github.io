# Quarterly Planning Readiness — Mockup Guide

**All information is fictional. This is an independent portfolio mockup, not a live company dashboard.**

## Purpose

Show whether quarterly plans are ready for that quarter’s execution, replacing spreadsheets, PowerPoint status slides, and disconnected views across planning and delivery sources.

## Read the hierarchy

1. Annual goals continue throughout the year.
2. Workstreams span multiple quarters and retain their goal alignment.
3. Demos belong to a specific quarter within a workstream.

## Explore

- Filter by annual goal or team. The overview, readiness measures, workstreams, and demos update together.
- Select a matrix score to open the corresponding readiness measure and its gap list. Selecting a goal-specific score also filters the view to that goal.
- Expand a measure to see its denominator, definition, goal breakdown, gaps, source, and as-of date.
- Compare Q2 (current) and Q1 (previous) workstream snapshots, or select one quarter.
- Select a workstream name or readiness score to view that quarter’s demos.
- Search demos, show only gaps, or open an demo to inspect its planning and delivery checks.
- Clear drilldown preserves the global filters. Reset restores the full demonstration.

## Freshness

The intended production workflow refreshes source data daily and exposes the as-of date of every source. This public mockup uses a fixed fictional snapshot: March 26, 2027, with delivery data dated March 25, 2027. There are no production connections, scheduled jobs, or network requests in the mockup.

## Measures

Business priority rank, complete demo planning fields, submitted dependencies, recorded dependency decisions, delivery epic links, prior-quarter close-out, and workstream execution checks. Scores are calculated from the fictional dataset. Combined scores use completed records / applicable records. See the on-page methodology and repository README for definitions and thresholds.
