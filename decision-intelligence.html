About this demo

Designed by Alison Whittaker to demonstrate portfolio visibility, decision-making, and follow-through. All initiatives, people, dates, and outcomes are fictional. The weekly update is assembled from this fixed scenario using a template; no live AI model or external integrations are used. Decisions are saved only in this browser when local storage is available. Recording a decision does not change initiative health or prove a delivery outcome.

Scenario snapshot: October 15, 2026. Independent demonstration; not an Intuit system.
