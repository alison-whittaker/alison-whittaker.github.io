About this demo & counting rules

All organizations, goals, people, initiatives, dates, and source records are fictional. This is one prototype with connected Product and Engineering spaces, backed by the same initiative and workstream records. Your business unit, goal, scope, product health, search, and planning-area selections stay in place when switching. Central portfolio Jira and dedicated team Jira locations remain separate linked sources. Workstream and initiative timing reflects this demo’s operating model, not a universal requirement. This independent prototype is not an Intuit system. There are no live integrations or AI requests. “Open source” displays a simulated source record; no company data is accessed.

Workstreams span 3 months or more and live in central Jira. Initiatives are bounded to one quarter and also live in central Jira. Initiatives break into epics in dedicated team Jira spaces, and epics break into user stories. Initiative dependency requests can be triaged by receiving teams and linked to delivery epics in their own Jira location. Those epics retain their own parent initiative and link back to the request. Demo Jira locations are unconfigured; source buttons show simulated records. Demo dependency conversion is session-only and never writes to Jira.

Engineering delivery uses linked fictional epics, dependency requests, deliverable evidence, and five release gates. Engineering health is maintained separately from product health. Only accepted deliverables and completed gates count as ready; deployment alone does not imply product launch. Shared filters remain active when switching perspectives. Engineering health filters epics and their related outputs; product totals remain product-only.

Each initiative belongs to one business unit, one goal, and one P0/P1 workstream. Initiative urgency is separate from workstream priority. The same initiative may appear in several planning areas; do not add area counts together. Quarter = Q4 2026 (October–December). Roadmap includes all quarters; Quarterly Plan includes only Q4 items; Launched includes Q4 actual launches; Dependencies includes items with linked dependencies; Plan Changes includes recorded changes; Prioritization Review includes Q4 items with a review flag.

Counts reflect matching initiatives. Workstream health uses matching initiatives: Off track takes precedence, then At risk, then Undefined, then On track. Goal health totals count initiatives, not goals. Scope state is assessed at initiative level; mixed workstreams show their scope breakdown.
