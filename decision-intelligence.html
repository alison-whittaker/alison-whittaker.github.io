About this demo & counting rules

All organizations, goals, people, initiatives, dates, and source records are fictional. This independent prototype is not an Intuit system. There are no live integrations or AI requests. “Open source” displays a simulated source record; no company data is accessed.

Each initiative belongs to one business unit, one goal, and one P0/P1 workstream. Initiative urgency is separate from workstream priority. The same initiative may appear in several planning areas; do not add area counts together. Quarter = Q4 2026 (October–December). Roadmap includes all quarters; Quarterly Plan includes only Q4 items; Launched includes Q4 actual launches; Dependencies includes items with linked dependencies; Plan Changes includes recorded changes; Prioritization Review includes Q4 items with a review flag.

Counts reflect matching initiatives. Workstream health uses matching initiatives: Off track takes precedence, then At risk, then Undefined, then On track. Goal health totals count initiatives, not goals. Scope state is assessed at initiative level; mixed workstreams show their scope breakdown.
